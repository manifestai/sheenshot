Background generator from a screenshot

Drop a screenshot and get a background made from its colors, as a mesh and a gradient. Download the result as PNG.

Turn on JavaScript to use the background generator.

How this demo works

  • The screenshot is scaled down to about 120 px, just to read its colors.
  • Whites, greys, blacks and very dark or very light colors are left out.
  • Vivid colors are grouped by hue. Up to 4 of the most frequent become the background.
  • Mesh: one color in each corner. Gradient: the first two colors, at 135°.
  • If the screenshot is almost all neutral, Sheenshot uses your default background.

Your image never leaves the browser. Everything is drawn right here, on your computer.
