Questions

Why does my Mac say it cannot verify the app?+

The trial version has not gone through Apple's verification yet; that comes with the App Store launch. The first time, open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. After that, the app opens normally.

What happens after 30 days?+

The app tells you the trial is over and stops capturing. Your screenshots stay on your Mac. Join the waitlist to hear when the final version is out.

Do my screenshots go to the cloud?+

No. Everything stays on your Mac, including the text Sheenshot reads from screenshots for search. See the privacy policy.

Which Macs does it run on?+

Any Mac running macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later.

Is Pro a subscription?+

No. It is a one-time purchase on the Mac App Store that unlocks Pro for good on your account.

I found a problem. How do I report it?+

Use the support page. If you can, attach a screenshot of what happened.
