Descriptions

One line

Sheenshot is a Mac app to capture your screen, make screenshots beautiful and find any of them by their text, with everything kept on your Mac.

One paragraph

Sheenshot is a screenshot app for Mac (macOS 14 or later) that lives in the menu bar. It captures an area, a window or the full screen and gets the screenshot ready to show, with backgrounds, padding, shadow and annotations. Clean Stage hides desktop icons with one shortcut, Screenshot colors builds the background from the screenshot's own colors, and Copy for AI copies the screenshot at 1×, with the longest side up to 1568 px, ready to paste into AI chats such as Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini. The Library finds any screenshot by its text, color or source app. With Pro, screenshots become step-by-step guides with a company's colors, font and logo. Everything stays on the Mac, with no account and no cloud. Pro is a one-time purchase, coming with the Mac App Store launch. Made in Brazil, in English and Portuguese.
