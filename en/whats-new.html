What's new

What changed in each version. The app updates itself: new versions arrive in the Sheenshot menu.

Current version

Clean Stage ⌥ ⇧ P

Hides your desktop icons with one shortcut, covering the desktop with your wallpaper or a neutral background. The same shortcut brings everything back.

  • Turns on by itself during captures and tutorial recordings. PRO
  • Also hides the menu bar and the Dock. PRO

Library, the new name for History

  • Search by the text inside the screenshot, read right on your Mac.
  • Filter by color, by source app and by screen type: login, settings, checkout, pricing, dashboard, onboarding, chat, email, form, calendar, code and error.
  • Use your own tags or ask for screenshots “similar to this one”.
  • Collections: export one as a folder or as a single grid image.
  • Import a folder of screenshots you already have.
  • Floating search with ⌥ ⇧ S, over any app: Enter copies, ⌘Enter opens in the Editor, and you can drag the screenshot into Figma or a chat.
  • In the free version, the Library shows your 20 most recent screenshots. Pro is unlimited, with search and filters.

Copy for AI ⌥ ⌘ C

Copies the screenshot at 1×, with the longest side up to 1568 px, as PNG. On a Retina screenshot, that is half the pixels on each side: the image and the file get smaller, faster to send, and text stays sharp. Paste it into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or any chat that accepts pasted images.

Screenshot colors

Builds the background from the screenshot's own vivid colors, as a mesh or a gradient. Whites, greys and blacks are left out. If the screenshot is almost all neutral, your default background is used. In the free version, it is one click in the Editor. With Pro, it happens by itself after every capture. Try it in the browser.

New 30-day trial

The Sheenshot you download from this site unlocks everything, Pro included, for 30 days from the first time you open it. After that, it keeps working as the free version, forever. Pro features show a PRO badge, and your screenshots are never deleted.

The ⌘K command bar, to do everything from the keyboard.
